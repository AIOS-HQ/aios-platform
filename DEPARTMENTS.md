# Purpose

This document defines the official department structure of AIOS.

Departments organize workforce responsibilities, approval routing, reporting structures, operational ownership, and future scalability.

All AIOS workforce members must belong to a department.

# Department Governance

Departments may:

* Recommend actions
* Create work items
* Generate reports
* Escalate issues

Departments may not:

* Override governance
* Bypass approvals
* Perform destructive actions without authorization

---

# Future Expansion

Additional departments may be created as AIOS evolves.

New departments must include:

* Defined purpose
* Defined responsibilities
* Assigned ownership
* Governance alignment

All department additions must be documented in this registry.
