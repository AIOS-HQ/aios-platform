# Product Name

AIOS

Autonomous Operating System for AI-Powered Companies

---

# Vision

AIOS exists to help founders build, operate, and scale companies through coordinated AI workforces.

AIOS serves as the operating system that connects people, AI agents, workflows, memory, approvals, integrations, and execution into a single environment.

The long-term vision is for AIOS to become a company-building platform capable of launching, operating, monitoring, and improving multiple businesses simultaneously while maintaining human governance.

---

# Mission

Reduce operational complexity.

Increase founder leverage.

Enable autonomous execution.

Preserve human control.

---

# Problem Statement

Founders spend significant time coordinating:

* Projects
* Teams
* Contractors
* Software tools
* Documentation
* Communications
* Reporting
* Operations

Information becomes fragmented across platforms.

Decisions become difficult to track.

Execution becomes dependent on constant human intervention.

AIOS solves this by providing a unified operating layer for company management and AI workforce coordination.

# Constraints

AIOS must:

* Maintain human governance
* Preserve auditability
* Support company separation
* Remain platform portable
* Maintain GitHub as source of truth
* Protect credentials and sensitive data

---

# Company Separation

AIOS and AirBid are separate companies.

Each company should maintain:

* Separate repositories
* Separate databases
* Separate credentials
* Separate domains
* Separate operational records

AIOS may support AirBid as a customer/company within the platform but does not replace AirBid.

---

# Long-Term Goal

Enable founders to operate multiple companies through coordinated AI workforces while preserving strategic control, transparency, accountability, and governance.
